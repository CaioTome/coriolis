# Capítulo 8: Criação de Setor (Sector Creation)

### 8.1 Passos para Geração Hexagonal de Setor

Todo jogo de ficção científica digno do nome precisa ser capaz de fornecer uma reserva de mundos alienígenas interessantes. Mesmo que a campanha se passe em uma única região restrita do espaço, os PJs eventualmente vão querer pegar uma nave espacial, traçar um salto hiperespacial e ver o que existe além da fronteira do mapa.

As regras a seguir foram projetadas para permitir que o Mestre do Jogo (MJ) crie rapidamente um setor estelar completo e pronto para jogar, repleto de mundos únicos, perigos, oportunidades de aventura e ganchos de campanha sandbox.

#### Passo 1: Mapear a Grade de Hexágonos (8x10)

Um setor estelar típico no *Stars Without Number* é desenhado em um mapa com uma grade de hexágonos de **8 colunas por 10 linhas** (totalizando 80 hexágonos, numerados de `0101` a `0810`).

- Cada hexágono representa aproximadamente **um parsec** (cerca de 3,26 anos-luz) de distância no espaço físico.
- A distância de um salto hiperespacial (*Drill*) com um motor FTL (*Spike Drive*) de Nível 1 (*Spike Drive 1*) cobre 1 hexágono por salto básico.
- Se preferir, você pode adaptar um mapa estelar tridimensional real ou fictício, convertendo a distância em anos-luz para hexágonos equivalentes.

#### Passo 2: Posicionar os Sistemas Estelares

Com a grade definida, o próximo passo é determinar a localização dos sistemas estelares habitados ou de interesse.

1. **Quantidade de Estrelas**: Um setor equilibrado possui geralmente entre **21 e 30 sistemas estelares** (cerca de 1 estrela a cada 3 hexágonos).
2. **Gerando Coordenadas**: Para posicionar cada estrela aleatoriamente, role `1d8` para a coluna e `1d10` para a linha. Se o hexágono sorteado já possuir uma estrela, role novamente ou coloque-a em um hexágono adjacente vago.
3. **Navegabilidade do Setor**: Certifique-se de que nenhuma estrela fique totalmente isolada a mais de 3 ou 4 hexágonos de distância do seu vizinho mais próximo. Caso contrário, naves equipadas apenas com *Spike Drive 1* ou *2* nunca conseguirão alcançar aquele sistema.

#### Passo 3: Estabelecer os Mundos Habitados Principais

Embora um sistema estelar possa conter múltiplos planetas, luas e cinturões de asteroides, para fins de criação inicial de setor, **cada sistema possui um Mundo Principal habitado** (*Primary World*). Os colonos originais naturalmente teriam buscado o mundo mais hospitaleiro do sistema para se estabelecerem.

Para cada Mundo Principal do setor, determine:
1. **Duas Tags de Mundo** (*World Tags*): Role ou escolha duas tags da tabela de 100 Tags de Mundo na Seção 8.2.
2. **Atmosfera**: Role na tabela da Seção 8.3.
3. **Temperatura**: Role na tabela da Seção 8.3.
4. **Biosfera**: Role na tabela da Seção 8.3.
5. **População**: Role na tabela da Seção 8.3.
6. **Nível Tecnológico** (*Tech Level - NT / TL*): Role na tabela da Seção 8.3.
7. **Nome e Descrição**: Dê um nome marcante ao planeta e escreva uma frase resumo que sintetize sua identidade visual e cultural.

#### Passo 4: Marcar Polidades e Rotas Comerciais

Identifique quais sistemas estelares possuem contato frequente entre si e trace linhas de comunicação e rotas comerciais:

- **Rotas de Salto**: Qualquer lacuna de 4 ou mais hexágonos sem estrelas intermediárias não pode ser atravessada por naves pós-Silêncio (TL4) sem o uso de motores *Spike Drive* avançados ou rotas pretech perdidas.
- **Polidades Interestelares**: Se dois ou mais mundos vizinhos compartilharem governos, alianças ou laços diplomáticos, desenhe uma fronteira ao redor desse grupo de sistemas. Lembre-se de que, no cenário pós-Grito (TL4), a maioria dos mundos tem dificuldade para sustentar invasões militares interestelares de grande escala, de modo que a maioria das polidades é formada por laços culturais, acordos comerciais ou hegemonias locais sobre planetas mais primitivos.
- **Sistemas Independentes**: Em muitos setores, não há impérios ou governos multi-sistemas. Cada planeta é uma entidade política isolada. Isso é ideal para PJs livres, pois escapar para o sistema vizinho significa sair da jurisdição legal do planeta anterior.

#### Passo 5: Detalhar os Mundos Importantes (Ficha de Mundo)

Não há necessidade de detalhar minuciosamente todos os 25 mundos do setor antes do início da campanha. Detalhe apenas o **mundo inicial** onde a campanha começará e seus **vizinhos imediatos**.

Para detalhar um mundo importante, siga este roteiro:
1. **Template Cultural**: Escolha uma inspiração cultural do mundo real (ex: toque latino-americano, colonos russos, bizantinos, cultura nômade, etc.). Isso ajuda a gerar nomes de NPCs, culinária, arquitetura e costumes locais rapidamente.
2. **Componentes de Aventura (Tags)**: Combine os Inimigos, Amigos, Complicações, Coisas e Lugares das duas Tags do planeta para criar NPCs, itens de interesse e ganchos de aventuras locais.
3. **5 Perguntas Fundamentais sobre o Planeta**:
   - *O que este mundo tem que atrai os PJs?* (Exporta recursos valiosos, tecnologia única, trabalhos bem pagos para aventureiros dispensáveis).
   - *Qual é o maior conflito atual do planeta?* (Luta de classes, tensões com comerciantes forasteiros, guerras entre facções locais).
   - *O que eles pensam sobre forasteiros?* (Receptivos, extremamente desconfiados, abertamente xenófobos).
   - *Como é a aplicação da lei?* (Leis corporativas estritas, polícia brutal, anarquia tolerada).
   - *Onde os PJs pousam?* (Nome e condições do principal porto estelar / starport do planeta).

#### Passo 6: Escolher Facções e Relações

Determine os relacionamentos entre os mundos e polidades do setor.
- **Guerra Fria e Espionagem**: Em vez de guerras abertas de frotas, a maioria dos conflitos entre mundos TL4 ocorre por meio de espionagem, sabotagem e guerras por procuração.
- **Facções de Cenário**: Crie de 2 a 3 Facções (organizações, corporações, impérios ou cultos) usando as regras do Capítulo 11 para dar movimento dinâmico ao cenário de fundo do setor.

### 8.2 Tags de Mundos (World Tags)

As **Tags de Mundos** (*World Tags*) são o elemento mais poderoso da geração de mundos em *Stars Without Number*. Elas são tropos conceituais rápidos que diferenciam um planeta de outros com população e clima semelhantes.

- Para cada mundo, role ou escolha **duas Tags** (`2d100`).
- Usar uma única tag pode deixar o planeta genérico; usar três ou mais pode deixar o conceito confuso.
- Cada Tag inclui listas de **Inimigos**, **Amigos**, **Complicações**, **Coisas** e **Lugares** que servem como matéria-prima imediata para o Mestre construir aventuras e NPCs.

#### Detalhamento das Tags de Mundos

### 01. Colônia Abandonada (Abandoned Colony)

The world once hosted a colony, whether human or otherwise, until some crisis or natural disaster drove the inhabitants away or killed them off. The colony might have been mercantile in nature, an expedition to extract valuable local resources, or it might have been a reclusive cabal of zealots. The remains of the colony are usually in ruins, and might still be dangerous from the aftermath of whatever destroyed it in the first place.

- **Inimigos**: Crazed survivors, Ruthless plunderers of the ruins, Automated defense system
- **Amigos**: Inquisitive stellar archaeologist, Heir to the col ony’s property, Local wanting the place cleaned out and made safe
- **Complicações**: The local government wants the ruins to remain a secret, The locals claim ownership of it, The colony is crumbling and dangerous to navigate
- **Coisas**: Long-lost property deeds, Relic stolen by the colonists when they left, Historical record of the colonization attempt
- **Lugares**: Decaying habitation block, Vine-covered town square, Structure buried by an ancient landslide

---

### 02. Humanidade Alterada (Altered Humanity)

The humans on this world are visibly and drastically different from normal humanity. They may have additional limbs, new sensory organs, or other significant changes. Were these from ancestral eugenic manipulation, strange stellar mutations, or from an environmental toxin unique to this world?

- **Inimigos**: Biochauvinist local, Local experimenter, Mentally unstable mutant
- **Amigos**: Local seeking a “cure”, Curious xenophiliac, Anthropological researcher
- **Complicações**: Alteration is contagious, Alteration is necessary for long-term survival, Locals fear and mistrust non-local humans
- **Coisas**: Original pretech mutagenic equipment, Valuable biological byproduct from the mutants, “Cure” for the altered genes, Record of the original colonial genotypes
- **Lugares**: Abandoned eugenics laboratory, An environment requiring the mutation for survival, A sacred site where the first local was transformed

---

### 03. Ruínas Alienígenas (Alien Ruins)

The world has significant alien ruins present. The locals may or may not permit others to investigate the ruins, and may make it difficult to remove any objects of value without substantial payment. Any surviving ruins with worthwhile salvage almost certainly have some defense or hazard to explain their unplundered state.

- **Inimigos**: Customs inspector, Worshipper of the ruins, Hidden alien survivor
- **Amigos**: Curious scholar, Avaricious local resident, Interstellar smuggler C Traps in the ruins, Remote location, Paranoid customs officials
- **Complicações**: 
- **Coisas**: Precious alien artifacts, Objects left with the remains of a prior unsuccessful expedition, Untranslated alien texts, Untouched hidden ruins
- **Lugares**: Undersea ruin, Orbital ruin, Perfectly preserved alien building, Alien mausoleum

---

### 04. Anarquistas (Anarchists)

Rather than being an incidental anarchy of struggling tribes and warring factions, this world actually has a functional society with no centralized authority. Authority might be hyper-localized to extended families, specific religious parishes, or voluntary associations. Some force is preventing an outside group or internal malcontents from coalescing into a power capable of imposing its rule on the locals; this force might be an ancient Pretech defense system, a benevolent military AI, or the sheer obscurity and isolation of the culture.

- **Inimigos**: Offworlder imperialist, Reformer seeking to impose “good government”, Exploiter taking advantage of the lack of centralized resistance
- **Amigos**: Proud missionary for anarchy, Casual local free spirit, Curious offworlder political scientist
- **Complicações**: The anarchistic structure is compelled by an external power, The anarchy is enabled by currently abundant resources, The protecting force that shelters the anarchy is waning
- **Coisas**: A macguffin that would let the possessor enforce their rule on others, A vital resource needed to preserve general liberty, Tech forbidden as disruptive to the social order
- **Lugares**: Community of similar-sized homes, Isolated clan homestead, Automated mining site

---

### 05. Antropomórficos (Anthropomorphs)

The locals were originally human, but at some point became anthropomorphic, either as an ancient furry colony, a group of animal-worshiping sectarians, or gengineers who just happened to find animal elements most convenient for surviving on the world. Depending on the skill of the original gengineers, their feral forms may or may not work as well as their original human bodies, or may come with drawbacks inherited from their animal elements.

- **Inimigos**: Anthro-supremacist local, Native driven by feral urges, Outside exploiter who sees the locals as subhuman creatures
- **Amigos**: Fascinated genetic researcher, Diplomat trained to deal with normals, Local needing outside help
- **Complicações**: The locals consider their shapes a curse from their foolish ancestors, Society is ordered according to animal forms, The locals view normal humans as repulsive or inferior
- **Coisas**: Pretech gengineering tech, A “cure” that may not be wanted, Sacred feral totem
- **Lugares**: Shrine to a feral deity, Nature preserve suited to an animal type, Living site built to take advantage of animal traits

---

### 06. Área 51 (Area 51)

The world’s government is fully aware of their local stellar neighbors, but the common populace has no idea about itand the government means to keep it that way. Trade with government officials in remote locations is possible, but any attempt to clue the commoners in on the truth will be met with lethal reprisals.

- **Inimigos**: Suspicious government minder, Free merchant who likes his local monopoly, Local who wants a specimen for dissection
- **Amigos**: Crusading offworld investigator, Conspiracy-theorist local, Idealistic government reformer
- **Complicações**: The government has a good reason to keep the truth concealed, The government ruthlessly oppresses the natives, The government is actually composed of offworlders
- **Coisas**: Elaborate spy devices, Memory erasure tech, Possessions of the last offworlder who decided to spread the truth
- **Lugares**: Desert airfield, Deep subterranean bunker, Hidden mountain valley

---

### 07. Mundo Desolado (Badlands) (Badlands World)

Whatever the original climate and atmosphere type, something horrible happened to this world. Biological, chemical, or nanotechnical weaponry has reduced it to a wretched hellscape. Some local life might still be able to survive on its blasted surface, usually at some dire cost in health or humanity.

- **Inimigos**: Mutated badlands fauna, Desperate local, Badlands raider chief
- **Amigos**: Native desperately wishing to escape the world, Scientist researching ecological repair methods, Ruin scavenger
- **Complicações**: Radioactivity, Bioweapon traces, Broken terrain, Sudden local plague
- **Coisas**: Maltech research core, Functional pretech weaponry, An uncontaminated well
- **Lugares**: Untouched oasis, Ruined city, Salt flat

---

### 08. Mestres das Feras (Beastmasters)

The natives have extremely close bonds with the local fauna, possibly having special means of communication and control through tech or gengineering. Local animal life plays a major role in their society, indus try, or warfare, and new kinds of beasts may be bred to suit their purposes.

- **Inimigos**: Half-feral warlord of a beast swarm, Coldly inhuman scientist, Altered beast with human intellect and furious malice
- **Amigos**: Native bonded with an adorable animal, Herder of very useful beasts, Animal-revering mystic
- **Complicações**: The “animals” are very heavily gengineered humans, The animals actually run the society, The animals have the same rights as humans
- **Coisas**: Tech used to alter animal life, A plague vial that could wipe out the animals, A pretech device that can perform a wonder if operated by a beast
- **Lugares**: Park designed as a comfortable home for beasts, Public plaza designed to accommodate animal companions, Factory full of animal workers

### 09. Campo de Batalha (Battleground)

The world is a battleground for two or more outside powers. They may be interstellar rivals, or groups operating out of orbitals or other system bodies. Something about the planet is valuable enough for them to fight over, but the natives are too weak to be anything but animate obstacles to the fight.

- **Inimigos**: Ruthless military commander, Looter pack chieftain, Traitorous collaborator
- **Amigos**: Native desperately seeking protection, Pragmatic military officer, Hapless war orphan
- **Complicações**: The war just ended as both sides are leaving, The natives somehow brought this on themselves, A small group of natives profit tremendously from the fighting
- **Coisas**: A cache of the resource the invaders seek, Abandoned prototype military gear, Precious spy intelligence lost by someone
- **Lugares**: Artillery-pocked wasteland, Reeking refugee camp, Burnt-out shell of a city

### 10. Cidades Bolha (Bubble Cities)

Whether due to a lack of atmosphere or an uninhabitable climate, the world’s cities exist within domes or pressurized buildings. In such sealed environments, techniques of surveillance and control can grow baroque and extreme.

- **Inimigos**: Native dreading outsider contamination, Saboteur from another bubble city, Local official hostile to outsider ignorance of laws
- **Amigos**: Local rebel against the city officials, Maintenance chief in need of help, Surveyor seeking new building sites
- **Complicações**: Bubble rupture, Failing atmosphere reprocessor, Native revolt against officials, All-seeing surveillance cameras
- **Coisas**: Pretech habitat technology, Valuable industrial products, Master key codes to a city’s security system
- **Lugares**: City power core, Surface of the bubble, Hydroponics complex, Warren-like hab block

### 11. Vida Barata (Cheap Life)

Human life is near-worthless on this world. Ubiquitous cloning, local conditions that ensure early death a culture that reveres murder, or a social structure that utterly discounts the value of most human lives ensures that death is the likely outcome for any action that irritates someone consequential.

- **Inimigos**: Master assassin, Bloody-handed judge, Overseer of disposable clones
- **Amigos**: Endearing local whose life the PCs accidentally bought, Escapee from death seeking outside help, Reformer trying to change local mores
- **Complicações**: Radiation or local diseases ensure all locals die before twenty-five years of age, Tech ensures that death is just an annoyance, Locals are totall convinced of a blissful afterlife
- **Coisas**: Device that revives or re-embodies the dead, Maltech engine fueled by human life, Priceless treasure held by a now-dead owner
- **Lugares**: Thronging execution ground, extremely cursory cemetery, Factory full of lethal dangers that could be corrected easily

### 12. Guerra Fria (Cold War)

Two or more great powers control the planet, and they have a hostility to each other that’s just barely less than open warfare. The hostility might be ideological in nature, or it might revolve around control of some local resource.

- **Inimigos**: Suspicious chief of intelligence, Native who thinks the outworlders are with the other side, Femme fatale
- **Amigos**: Apolitical information broker, Spy for the other side, Unjustly accused innocent, “He’s a bastard, but he’s our bastard” official
- **Complicações**: Police sweep, Low-level skirmishing, “Red scare”
- **Coisas**: List of traitors in government, secret military plans, Huge cache of weapons built up in preparation for war
- **Lugares**: Seedy bar in a neutral area, Political rally, Isol ed area where fighting is underway

The world is currently torn between at least two op, posing factions, all of which claim legitimacy. The war may be the result of a successful rebel uprising against tyranny, or it might just be the result of schemers who plan to be the new masters once the revolution is complete.

- **Inimigos**: Faction commissar, Angry native, Conspiracy theorist who blames offworlders for the war, Deserter looking out for himself, Guerrilla bandit chieftain
- **Amigos**: Faction loyalist seeking aid, Native caught in the crossfire, Offworlder seeking passage off the planet y C The front rolls over the group, Famine strikes, Bandit infestations are in the way

### 15. Potência Cultural (Cultural Power)

The world is a considerable cultural power in the sector, producing music, art, philosophy, or some similar intangible that their neighbors find irresistibly attractive. Other worlds might have a profound degree of cultural cachet as the inheritor of some venerable artistic tradition.

- **Inimigos**: Murderously eccentric artist, Crazed fan, Failed artist with an obsessive grudge, Critic with a crusade to enact
- **Amigos**: Struggling young artist, Pupil of the artistic tra dition, Scholar of the art, Offworlder hating the source of corrupting alien ways
- **Complicações**: The art is slowly lethal to its masters, The art is mentally or physically addictive, The art is a fragment of ancient technical or military science

### 16. Ciborgues (Cyborgs)

The planet’s population makes heavy use of cybernetics, with many of the inhabitants possessing at least a cosmetic amount of chrome. This may be the result of a strong local cyber tech base, a religious injunction, or simply a necessary measure to survive the local conditions.

- **Inimigos**: Ambitious hacker of cyber implants, Cybertech oligarch, Researcher craving fresh offworlders, Cybered-up gang boss
- **Amigos**: Charity-working implant physician, Idealistic young cyber researcher, Avant-garde activist
- **Complicações**: The powerful and dangerous come here often for cutting-edge implants, The cyber has some universal negative side-effect, Cyber and those implanted with it are forbidden to leave the planet as a tech security measure
- **Coisas**: Unique prototype cyber implant, Secret research files, A virus that debilitates cyborgs, A cache o critically-needed therapeutic cyber
- **Lugares**: Grimy slum chop-shop, Bloody lair of implant rippers, Stark plaza where everyone is seeing things through their augmented-reality cyber

### 17. Cibercomunistas (Cybercommunists)

On this world communism actually works, thanks to Pretech computing devices and greater or lesser amounts of psíquico precognition. Central planning nodes direct all production and employment on the world. Citizens in good standing have access to ample amounts of material goods for all needs and many wants. Instead of strife over wealth, conflicts erupt over political controls, cultural precepts, or control over the planning nodes. Many cybercommunist worlds show a considerable bias toward the private interests of those who run the planning nodes.

- **Inimigos**: Embittered rebel against perceived unfairness, Offworlder saboteur, Aspiring Stalin-figure
- **Amigos**: Idealistic planning node tech, Cynical anti-corruption cop, Precognitive economist
- **Complicações**: The pretech planning computers are breaking down, The planning only works because the locals have been mentally or physically altered, The planning computers can’t handle the increasing population within the system
- **Coisas**: Planning node computer, Wildly destabilizing commodity that can’t be factored into plans, A tremendous store of valuables made by accident
- **Lugares**: Humming factory, Apartment block of perfectly equal flats, Mass demonstration of unity

### 18. Perdição Cíclica (Cyclical Doom)

The world regularly suffers some apocalyptic catastrophe that wipes out organized civilization on it.
